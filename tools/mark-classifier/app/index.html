Ink roles

No selection

Labels are drafts until approved. Unlabelled and draft ink is excluded from training.

Dots & marks pilot

Label dots and marks without identifying any letters. Use Other ink for a reviewed negative example.

For one mark made of several regions, Shift-select them and use Merge regions before labelling. Letter ownership can be added later.

Character & form

Each letter cut belongs to a character instance. Body, connection, dots and marks share its details.

Choose the letter that owns the selected ink. For a join, choose the preceding letter in reading order. Approve each cut after checking it.

Body/connection ink requires a character and position. Dots and marks can be reviewed independently.

Initial/final describe joining, not the word index. For another letter after a split, choose New character.

Subword context

Correct vector proposals or create a subword manually. Its body can contain several uncut letters.

Subword targets are separate from individual letter targets.
